**SnapSpot is not a map of foraging areas.** It does not tell you where to go: it helps you scout, then keeps the
memory of your own spots. Mushrooms come back year after year, in the same place at the same time: yesterday's spots
prepare tomorrow's season.

## Identify, with a model made for mushrooms

Identification relies on a **specialised model**, trained on thousands of photos of mushrooms from France and
embedded in the app. It is based on BioCLIP, a model for recognising living things from the Imageomics Institute.

<p class="video"><video src="../images/identification-direct.mp4" poster="../images/identification-direct-poster.jpg" controls muted playsinline preload="none"></video></p>

*Live: the app analyses what the camera is pointed at, suggests species, flags dangerous look-alikes, then creates the
spot.*

- **Over 200 species.** The app does not know them all: some deadly species are missing for lack of photos, and the
  app reminds you of it.
- **On our test photos**, the right species comes first in about 9 cases out of 10, and among the top three
  suggestions in nearly 98 % of cases. The app always shows several suggestions and the criteria to check, never a
  verdict.
- **Live** with the camera, or on photos you have already taken.
- **Everything on the iPhone**: no image is sent over the Internet, and it works deep in the forest, without a
  network.
- **Apple Intelligence** (compatible iPhones) only provides a description of what the photo shows, compared with the
  criteria of the species sheet. It never names the species: the SnapSpot model makes the suggestions.
- **Toxic or deadly species are always flagged.** You can also hide inedible species from the map.

## Scout and find your spots again

IGN maps are downloaded in advance: aerial photographs, plan, relief, forest map, public forests, ancient forests,
cadastre, slopes, hydrography, contour lines, geology. A long press on the map tells you what the layer shows at that
spot.

- **Soil map (INRAE)** next to the forest layers: the soil type, with its legend, can be read with a long press.
- **Each layer has its own opacity**, and a global slider lets you see the satellite photo through them.

## Remember your finds

Every geotagged photo becomes a spot. Browse them as a grid or a list, filter by period, species, year or edibility, group the photos of the same mushroom, drop markers (car, water point, hut, remarkable tree, viewpoint) and add photos from your library, an album or another iPhone. Each photo keeps its place, its date, the weather of the previous days and an indicative growing-conditions index.

## Return to a spot

Guidance takes you on foot to a spot: distance, direction, walking time, and in augmented reality a sonar on the
ground showing the area. With the screen locked, beeps and vibrations tell you whether you are heading the right way,
and a voice can correct your course.

<p class="video"><video src="../images/guidage-ra.mp4" poster="../images/guidage-ra-poster.jpg" controls muted loop playsinline preload="none"></video></p>

*The sonar: a wave leaves the spot and widens along the ground, at the real size of the area, to judge the distance.*

- **Three views**: map, radar compass or augmented reality, with a drawer you pull up for details or minimise to keep an eye on the map.
- **Several points in a row**: chain spots into a route, or drop points on the map and let SnapSpot guide you from one to the next.

## Walk and create your own routes

- **GPS tracking** of your walks: distance, elevation, speed, pauses and markers, even with the screen locked.
- **Your own routes**: chain your finds or drop points on the map, and SnapSpot guides you from point to point.
- **Return to your start** in a straight line, along your own track or along trails. Only “By trails” contacts IGN's routing service, with your position and destination, never your photos or spots.
- **Recording drawer**: time, distance, speed and markers at a glance, pause, stop and “Back to start” under your thumb.
- **Car detection**: if you forget to stop the recording in the car, SnapSpot offers to cut the end of the track.
- **Sunset reminder**: an alert when it is time to head back, so that night does not catch you out.

## Hikes

A catalogue of nearly 38,000 routes in France (OpenStreetMap, IGN) is built into the app and can be browsed offline: distance, elevation gain, altitude profile, duration and difficulty, calculated by the app and given for information only.

- **Search by place and filters** on the routes of an area, on a map and in a list.
- **Start**: SnapSpot records your walk and guides you along the route, with voice prompts at the turns and an alert if you leave the trail. The recording can be switched off.
- **My routes**: draw your own by hand or point by point, snapped to the trails of the IGN network (network required), then share it with another iPhone or export it as GPX.

## A simple interface that adapts to you

SnapSpot's interface was redesigned around the way people use a phone in the forest: one hand, little time, sometimes a weak signal.

- **Five tabs**: Map, Finds, Identify (the big button in the middle), Walks and Species.
- **Simple or Expert**: in Simple, details are folded behind a small button; in Expert, they are unfolded. All features and all data stay available in both, and safety warnings are never folded away. You can switch at any time.
- **Light or dark, portrait or landscape**: the interface follows the iPhone, and the screen orientation can be forced in the settings.
- **Built-in help**: a guided tour, a help centre by theme with illustrations, and tips that show up only once.

## Absolute privacy

- **No SnapSpot server, no account, no ads, no tracking, no usage statistics.**
- Your photos, spots, tracks and analyses **stay on your iPhone**. We cannot see them, even if we wanted to.
- Identification, description and guidance happen **on the device**.
- Only public map and weather services are contacted (IGN maps, place names, place search, rain over the last 14
  days), like any web page, and never with your photos or spots. Maps downloaded in advance work without a network.
- Your mushroom spots are yours: they leave the iPhone only if you share them yourself.

## Also in the app

- **Field clues**: note the smell, the flesh, the habitat or the spore print, by dictation or from suggestions. They
  weight the suggestions and an Apple Intelligence comparative opinion, which you can rerun.
- **Search on the map**: a place, an address, coordinates, or one of your finds, a track, a marker.
- **Rain and moon**: rain over the last 14 days and soil moisture on the “Here” sheet, and today's moon phase, for
  information only.
- **Growing conditions**: an indicative index drawn from rain, soil moisture and soil temperature (network required), never a promise of a harvest.
- **Sharing from Photos**: the iPhone share sheet sends photos to SnapSpot, which reads their location.
- **Export and transfer**: GPX, spots, tracks with their photos, or your whole collection to another iPhone by
  AirDrop.
- **Health app**: your walks can be saved there as hikes (write only).
- **Species sheets**: over 200, with seasons, habitats, freely licensed photos and credits.
- **Nine languages**: French, English, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish and Swedish.

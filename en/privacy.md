# SnapSpot privacy policy

*Last updated: 31 October 2026*

SnapSpot is an iOS app developed by Ponin Sor (“we”). This page explains which data the app uses, where it stays,
and which services it contacts.

## In short

- **We collect no data.** There is no account, no advertising, no tracking, no usage statistics and no third-party
  analytics tool.
- Your photos, spots, tracks, markers and sheets stay **on your iPhone**.
- Nothing is sent to us: we have no server.

## Data used on your iPhone

This data is processed only on the device, to make the app work. Each access is subject to your permission, which you
can withdraw at any time in Settings › Apps › SnapSpot.

**Location**
To show your position on the map, guide you to a spot, place a spot and record your tracks. In the background,
location is used only while recording a track you started (and to warn you if you seem to have forgotten to stop it).

**Photos**
SnapSpot reads the date and location of your foraging photos to place them on the map, and keeps thumbnails in the
app. Photos taken in SnapSpot are added to your photo library if you agree. If you send photos to SnapSpot from the
iPhone share sheet, they pass through a folder shared between the share extension and the app, on the device, and are
then imported into the app.

**Camera**
To photograph a find, identify a mushroom live and display augmented reality guidance. Camera images are analysed on
the iPhone and are not kept, except the photo you take.

**Species recognition and Apple Intelligence**
Species recognition uses a model built into the app. Photo description uses Apple Intelligence on the device. No image
is sent over the Internet for these analyses.

**Motion and fitness**
The barometer and pedometer measure the elevation gain and step count of your tracks.

**Health (HealthKit)**
If you enable the option, your walks are saved in the Health app as hikes, with their route. SnapSpot **reads no
Health data**. Data written to Health is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

**Notifications**
Local notifications, created on the iPhone, remind you of a track still running or a possible car trip with an active
track. No notification goes through a server. The lock screen shows the progress of the track in a Live Activity,
managed by the iPhone.

**Sound and voice**
Guidance beeps and the voice of the co-pilot are produced on the iPhone. SnapSpot does not use the microphone.

## Services contacted by the app

For some features, the app queries public services. As with any web page, these services see your IP address and the
requested area; they receive no other information about you.

- **IGN – Géoplateforme** (data.geopf.fr): maps, aerial photographs, forest layers, cadastre, geology (BRGM) and
  slopes. The “Here” feature (long press on the map) sends the position of the touched point to find out what the
  layer shows there.
- **OpenStreetMap** and **OpenTopoMap**: only if you choose these map backgrounds.
- **Apple Maps**: Apple map backgrounds, and place names (forest, municipality) derived from the location of your
  photos and tracks, as well as the places and addresses you type in the map search. These requests are handled by
  Apple according to its own privacy policy.
- **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com): weather for the last 14 days (rain, temperatures, soil moisture and temperature),
  shown on the map's “Here” sheet and in the detail of a find. The position of the touched point or of the find,
  rounded to about 1 km, and the date of the find are sent, only when a network is available; the last weather
  consulted is kept on the device.
- **Wikipedia**: an article opens in your browser only if you tap its link.

Maps already viewed and downloaded areas stay on the iPhone for offline use.

## This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub may record technical logs (IP address, requested page)
when you visit it. SnapSpot adds no tracking and no cookies there.

## Sharing

A spot, a track or a backup leaves your iPhone only if you share it yourself (AirDrop, Messages, Files…), with the
person or app of your choice.

## Retention, backup and deletion

- Your data stays on the iPhone until you delete it, in the app or by uninstalling it. **Deleting the app erases all
  its data from the device.**
- It is included in the iPhone's iCloud backup, if you use one, managed by Apple. Caches that can be recomputed are not
  included.
- Hikes saved in Health are deleted from the Health app.

## Your rights

We hold no data about you: your rights of access, rectification and erasure (General Data Protection Regulation) are
exercised directly on your iPhone, by editing or deleting your data in the app. You can withdraw each permission in
Settings › Apps › SnapSpot. For any question, write to us (see below). You can also contact your data protection
authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).

## Children

SnapSpot collects no data, including from children.

## Changes

If the app changes (new feature, new service contacted), this page will be updated and its date changed.

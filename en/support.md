# SnapSpot support

**Contact**: snapspot.app@outlook.com. Please state your iPhone model, the iOS version and, if possible, what you
were doing when the problem occurred.

## Getting started

**How do I add photos?**
From the Photos tab: the app's camera, import from your photo library or an album, or the **Share** button of the
Photos or Files app, which offers SnapSpot. Only photos with a GPS location are placed on the map; a photo received
through the share sheet without a location is discarded, and the app tells you so.

**My photos do not appear on the map.**
Check that SnapSpot has access to your photos (Settings › Apps › SnapSpot › Photos) and that the camera records the
location (Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Camera).

**What is the heat map for?**
Each area groups nearby photos. The darker it is, the more finds you have there. These are your finds, not other
people's: SnapSpot does not provide foraging areas.

## Maps and offline

**Does the app work without a network?**
Yes: already downloaded maps, tracks, guidance, species sheets and identification work offline. Place names are added
when the network returns.

**How do I get IGN maps in the forest?**
Download them before you leave, preferably over Wi-Fi: Settings › Map › Offline maps. Choose the background and the
layers (forest map, public forests, ancient forests…), then the area: the visible area or “Around my spots”.

**What do the layers show?**
A long press on the map tells you what the layer shows at that spot. OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap backgrounds cannot
be downloaded in advance: only areas you have already viewed remain available.

## Identification

**Is identification reliable?**
It is **indicative**. The model gives several suggestions with a percentage, and the app flags toxic or deadly species
and their look-alikes. It does not know every species, and some deadly species are missing. Never eat a mushroom on
the strength of the app.

**What does Apple Intelligence do?**
It describes what the photo shows (colours, shape, stem…) on compatible iPhones. It never names the species. Without
Apple Intelligence, identification still works.

**The camera does not open.**
Allow it in Settings › Apps › SnapSpot › Camera.

## Guidance and tracks

**The track stops when the screen is locked.**
Allow location (Settings › Apps › SnapSpot › Location) set to “While Using” or “Always”. If the Live Activity shows
“Recording interrupted?”, open the app: the track is recovered.

**Guidance makes no sound when the screen is locked.**
Guidance keeps the position for 4 minutes by default after locking (adjustable from 1 to 30 minutes in Settings ›
Guidance to a spot). Also check that the sound button on the guidance screen is not muted.

**How do the guidance sounds work?**
A heading beep tells you whether you are going the right way (higher-pitched when you stray, stereo or spatial sound
with compatible AirPods), then an approach sonar speeds up as you get closer. Settings › Guidance to a spot ›
“Understanding the sounds” explains them through play.

**Augmented reality does not start.**
Allow the camera. Otherwise, switch to compass mode: Settings › Guidance to a spot.

## Your data

**How do I move my data to another iPhone?**
Photos tab › Import or export menu › Transfer to another iPhone: prepare the file, then send it by AirDrop. It opens
in SnapSpot on the other iPhone. A track can also be exported with its photos (track detail › Actions) or as GPX.

**How do I delete my data?**
In the app (Photos and Tracks tabs, Settings › Storage), or by deleting the app, which erases everything. See the
[privacy policy](privacy).

## Safety

Identification is **indicative** and can be wrong, including between an edible species and a deadly one. Never eat a
mushroom on the strength of the app: have it checked by a pharmacist or a mycological society.

If you feel unwell after eating mushrooms: call your local emergency number (**112** in Europe, **15** in France) or a
poison control centre. Keep the leftovers and a photo of the find.
